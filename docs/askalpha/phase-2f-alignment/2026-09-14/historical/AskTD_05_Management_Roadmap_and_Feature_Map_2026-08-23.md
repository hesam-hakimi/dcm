## Foundation delivered through Phase 2E

askAlpha now has enterprise access controls, read-only bounded execution, versioned governed metadata, provider-neutral architecture, a governed Approved Recipe pilot, exact field dependencies, change fingerprints, and fail-closed impact detection.

Phase 2E was merged and independently verified on 2026-08-26. Its automated workflows completed successfully with no failed jobs.

## Phase 2F — Lifecycle, certified semantics, templates, relationship impact

Phase 2F makes governed answers operationally manageable:

- show whether a recipe is valid, requires review, is broken, or lacks approval;
- explain which governed change caused the status;
- support certified KPI names, glossary terms, and business rules;
- define reusable output templates for consistent reports;
- add governed relationship impact only when relationship evidence is available.

The recommended first delivery is recipe lifecycle evaluation. KPI/glossary, templates, and relationships follow as separately reviewed slices.

## Later roadmap

- Phase 3: approved Databricks and other providers; Genie coexistence boundary.
- Phase 4: joins, grain/cardinality, duplicate-counting, and data-safety controls.
- Phase 5: audit, traces, answer-quality evaluation, and regression monitoring.
- Phase 6: benchmark-driven performance and scope-aware caching; Redis only if justified.
- Phase 7: governed self-service onboarding, approval, and publishing.
- Phase 8: richer visualization, reports, downloads, exports, and integrations.

## Management takeaway

The platform has completed the foundation needed to detect when a governed business answer is affected by metadata change. The next step is to turn that evidence into an explicit, reviewable lifecycle before expanding providers, datasets, and self-service.
