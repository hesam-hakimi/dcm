## Key decisions / retained direction

- Start with Deposits, bounded users and approved questions; keep autonomous actions and advanced what-if analysis outside the initial pilot.
- Use available approved infrastructure and qualify the preferred UC-governed data route; do not make serverless availability or a full enterprise ontology prerequisites.
- Retain MSI source access with application enforcement and no custom ACL synchronization. The enterprise entitlement authority remains an open dependency.
- Maintain a provider-independent semantic/runtime architecture. Preserve enterprise ownership of business definitions and versioned Registry references in templates.
- Treat caching and streaming separately. AMR/streaming is not an MVP 1 requirement; optimize bounded reuse based on actual workload evidence.
- Establish business evaluation and model/trace qualification as pilot acceptance evidence.

The above are delivery and working-design directions. They do not confirm new platform approvals, final owner assignments or a framework migration.
